__signature__
keras.ops.erfc(x)
__doc__
Computes the complementary error function of `x`, element-wise.

Args:
    x: Input tensor.

Returns:
    A tensor with the same dtype as `x`.

Example:
>>> x = np.array([-3.0, -2.0, -1.0, 0.0, 1.0])
>>> keras.ops.erfc(x)
array([1.999978 , 1.9953222, 1.8427008, 1. , 0.1572992],
  dtype=float32)
